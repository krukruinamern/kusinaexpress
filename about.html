OUR ORIGIN

A Legacy Passed Down Through Generations

Kusina Express began with our grandmother's lifelong passion for cooking and her love for preparing family meals. What started as a simple home kitchen gradually grew into a proud, family-owned business, passing down heirloom recipes and culinary traditions from one generation to the next. Situated in an area surrounded by hardworking locals and students, we set out with a clear purpose: to serve hot, delicious, and satisfying home-cooked food at prices everyone could afford.

Building our reputation wasn't easy amidst tough local competition, but our commitment to consistent quality and warm service slowly won over our community. Today, our growth is powered by the loyal support and recommendations of our customers. More than just serving meals, we strive to make everyone feel welcomed and at home, ensuring that every bite delivers the comforting taste of tradition prepared with genuine care.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got questions? We've got clear answers for you.

Operations & Logistics

What are your operating hours?
We are open Monday through Saturday from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays.
Do you accept GCash or MariBank?
Yes! You can scan our GCash or MariBank QR codes on the wall to pay with your phone.
Is parking available?
We only have limited street parking out front, as our shop is built mostly for local foot traffic and neighbors walking over.
Do you offer delivery or advanced ordering?
Yes! We offer an advanced ordering system directly through our website. You can place your order online ahead of time for a quick and convenient experience.

Menu & Food Preparation

What is your menu for today?
We don't have a printed menu because our dishes change daily based on what is fresh at the market; just point at our glass display ("turo-turo") to pick what you want!
Do you serve corporate or bulk orders (pakyawan)?
We accept bulk orders for your parties, fiestas, or office meetings; just let us know 1 to 2 days in advance so we can prepare large catering trays for you.
Are there vegetarian or healthy options?
We cook mostly pork, beef, and chicken, but we always make sure to have vegetable sides available.
Is your food Halal?
We are not Halal-certified and we use pork in many of our recipes, so please keep that in mind if you have strict dietary restrictions.

Dining Experience & Policies

Is service water free?
We provide service water completely for free; you can help yourself to our water pitcher or dispenser anytime you get thirsty.
Can we reserve tables?
We strictly follow first-come, first-served and we do not take reservations; during our busy lunch rush, you might even share a table with a friendly stranger (salo-salo).
Is there a corkage fee for outside food or drinks?
We don't mind if you bring a drink from the nearby sari-sari store, but we might charge a small corkage fee if you bring a whole birthday cake or outside meals into our dining area.
